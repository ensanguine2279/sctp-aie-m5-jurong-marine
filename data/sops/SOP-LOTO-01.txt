JME SOP-LOTO-01 — LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE (rev 4, Jan 2026)
1. Identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, gravity).
2. Notify affected personnel and shift supervisor.
3. Shut down machine using normal stop procedure.
4. Isolate at main breaker; apply personal lock + tag. One lock per person working.
5. Verify zero energy: attempt restart, test with multimeter (electricians only).
6. For CNC machines: also release residual hydraulic pressure via manual valve (see machine manual).
7. After work: ensure all guards restored, all personnel clear, remove locks (owner only), notify supervisor.
VIOLATION = immediate stop-work + incident report. No exceptions, including 'quick jobs'.
