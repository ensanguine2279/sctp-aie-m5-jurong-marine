JME SOP-CONFINED-03 — CONFINED SPACE ENTRY (rev 2, Sep 2025)
Applies to: blasting chamber (BLAST-01), ballast tanks on client vessels, test pump sump.
- Entry permit required from WSH officer. Valid 8 hours max.
- Gas test before entry AND every 2 hours: O2 19.5-23.5%, LEL <10%, H2S <10ppm, CO <30ppm.
- Attendant stationed outside at all times with communication.
- MOM notification required for vessel tank entry (Shipyard regs).
